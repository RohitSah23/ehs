# whatisehs × HaloCPR: CPR Reel Series

**Page:** @whatisehs (EHS: Environment, Health & Safety). Bio: "Workplace safety, explained in 40 seconds."
**Product:** HaloCPR (https://halocpr.com)
**Length:** under 55 seconds per reel
**Goal:** teach one life-saving skill per reel, build awareness, and send viewers, workplaces and schools to HaloCPR.

---

## 1. Product facts (from halocpr.com)

- **What it is:** a CPR training app that runs in the browser at **app.halocpr.com**. No download, works on any phone.
- **How it works:** you practise on a manikin or a firm cushion. The phone camera and on-device AI check compression depth, rate, arm lock, posture and hand position. Audio cues keep you at 100–120 BPM, and the AI checks 13 error types. You get a score at the end.
- **Privacy:** no video is uploaded or recorded, and no data leaves the device.
- **Sold to:** schools (CPR lesson for ages 11–16) and organisations (hospitals, plants, remote sites). Individuals can use the app directly.
- **Taglines:** "Train CPR. Save Lives." / "CPR training that fits in a lunch break." / "Every student should know CPR."
- **CTAs:** "Start training" → app.halocpr.com. Schools and organisations → "Book a 15-min walkthrough" (halocpr.com/contact).
- **Never claim:** that it gives certification. The site says: "It is a training record, not an accredited CPR certification."

## 2. The format (9 beats, under 55 s)

| # | Beat | Time | What happens | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **HOOK** | 0–3 s | An emergency at work, and a clock starts | Under 12 words. Workplace setting |
| 1b | **DM CTA** | 3–5 s | "Comment HALO and we'll DM you…" | One quick line, spoken and on screen. Keyword is always **HALO** |
| 2 | **FEAR** | 5–8 s | What's lost every minute | One real fact |
| 3 | **LESSON TEASE** ⭐ | 8–11 s | Name the mistake, don't explain it | "And this is where almost everyone gets it wrong…" |
| 4 | **LAST PART'S ANSWER** | 11–14 s | Real % from comments, then the correct answer | Real numbers only. No beat 4 in Part 1. **No vote? Use the no-vote version (see 2c)** |
| 5 | **STORY** | 14–36 s | The crowd panics, you act, step by step | Jokes about the bystanders only, never the patient |
| 6 | **LESSON** ⭐ | 36–44 s | The skill, with exact numbers | Simple words, medically correct |
| 7 | **PRODUCT LINE** | 44–48 s | One HaloCPR line, app shown on screen | Always pitched as "practise", never "certify" |
| 8 | **COMMENT TRIGGER** | 48–55 s | Quiz with two options, answer revealed next part, then a tag CTA | Both options spoken and shown on screen |

**Why beats 3 and 6 matter most:** beat 3 promises a lesson, so viewers stay to hear it. Beat 6 delivers it, and that is what gets the reel saved and shared.

**Why the DM CTA comes early (beat 1b):** many viewers drop off before the end, so asking at second 3 catches them while they are hooked. It is kept to one line so it doesn't slow the story down.

**Two comment asks per reel:** HALO (beat 1b) gets the DM. The quiz word (beat 8) answers the question. Both push comments, which helps reach.

**Why beat 8 is a quiz:** medical questions have one correct answer. Revealing it in the next part brings viewers back and teaches a second skill.

## 2b. Comment-to-DM setup

- **Keyword:** HALO. It is the same in every reel, so the auto-DM tool needs only one rule. It never clashes with the quiz words (LEFT/CENTRE, STOP/SWITCH…).
- **The DM message:**
  > Here's your link to practise CPR on your phone, no download needed: https://app.halocpr.com
  > Your phone camera checks your depth and speed as you go. Save this, and send it to someone who should know CPR. 🫀
  > Want it for your school or workplace? Book a 15-min walkthrough: https://halocpr.com/contact
- **Tool:** any Instagram comment-to-DM automation (e.g. ManyChat). Set it to trigger on the word "HALO" in comments on all reels, and auto-reply to the comment with "Sent! Check your DMs 📩".
- **Caption line:** "Comment HALO for the practice link 👇"

## 2c. When there's no vote (optional beat 4)

The % bars only work when enough people answered the quiz. Pick the version for beat 4 like this:

| Quiz comments on last part | Use | How it sounds |
|---|---|---|
| **20 or more** | **Vote version**: real % bars, winner pops | "In Part 1, 64% of you said CENTRE. Correct…" |
| **1–19** | **Shout-out version**: name 1–3 people who answered, no % | "Riya and Arjun said CENTRE. Correct…" (their @handles on screen) |
| **0** | **No-vote version**: replay the question, reveal the answer | "Last time I asked: left side or centre? Answer: CENTRE…" |

**Rules:**
- Never invent a percentage or a commenter. If the numbers aren't real, use the no-vote version.
- Ask before naming anyone. Use only public @handles from the comments.
- The no-vote version is about the same length (3–4 s), so the timings don't change.
- The question is replayed with both options on screen, so a new viewer still follows.
- The "20 or more" cut-off is a starting point. Raise it once the page grows.

## 3. Series rules

- One skill per reel. Every setting is a workplace or a school.
- Everything on screen is also spoken. Both quiz options appear as text.
- HaloCPR appears only in beats 7–8. Part 5 is the spotlight, where it gets more time.
- The CTA tag rotates: HR team, safety officer, office, lunch group, school.
- Use real comment numbers only for beat 4.
- A doctor or the client signs off every medical line before posting.
- Versions are never overwritten. Save as `PartN_v1`, `PartN_v2`…

## 4. Quiz chain

| Part | Setting | Skill | Quiz at end | Answer (next part) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Office desk | Check response + breathing, call 112 | Hands: LEFT side or CENTRE of chest? | **CENTRE**, lower half of the breastbone |
| 2 | Factory floor | Hands-only CPR | Arms tired: STOP or SWITCH? | **SWITCH** about every 2 min, with minimal pause |
| 3 | Office lobby | Using an AED | After the shock: WAIT or PUSH? | **PUSH**, restart compressions straight away |
| 4 | Canteen | Choking (adult) | Alone and choking: WAIT or SELF? | **SELF**: call 112, abdominal thrusts with your fist or against a chair back |
| 5 | School staff room | Why everyone should train (HaloCPR spotlight) | Practise CPR on a pillow: YES or NO? | **YES**, a firm cushion works for practice. Never on a real person |

## 5. Scripts

### Part 1: "The Gasp" (Office)

1. **HOOK:** "Your colleague just collapsed at his desk. The clock is running."
   - **DM CTA:** "Comment HALO and we'll DM you a link to practise this on your phone."
   - *On screen:* Comment **HALO** → link in DM 📩
2. **FEAR:** "Every minute without CPR, his chance of survival drops by up to 10%."
3. **LESSON TEASE:** "And there's one sound that fools almost everyone…"
4. **STORY:**
   - "Someone shouts his name. Someone offers water. Someone says, 'Let him rest.'"
   - "You tap his shoulders hard. 'Can you hear me?' Nothing."
   - "Phone on speaker. 112. Then you watch his chest for ten seconds."
   - "He makes a slow, snoring gasp. Everyone relaxes. 'He's breathing.'"
5. **LESSON:** "He isn't. Slow gasps are not normal breathing. They're a sign the heart has stopped. No response plus no normal breathing means: start CPR now."
6. **PRODUCT LINE:** "Knowing it is step one. Practising it is step two. HaloCPR coaches you through your phone camera. No download."
   - *On screen:* HaloCPR logo + app.halocpr.com
7. **COMMENT TRIGGER:** "Now: when you start CPR, do your hands go on the left side, over the heart… or the centre of the chest? Comment LEFT or CENTRE. Answer in Part 2. And tag your HR team."
   - *On screen:* LEFT / CENTRE

### Part 2: "Push Like You Mean It" (Factory floor)

1. **HOOK:** "A worker drops on the factory floor. The ambulance is 15 minutes away."
   - **DM CTA:** "Comment HALO, and we'll DM you how to check your CPR speed on your phone."
   - *On screen:* Comment **HALO** → link in DM 📩

3. **LESSON TEASE:** "And most people push the wrong way…"

5. **STORY:**

   - "One… two… three… The supervisor starts counting with you."
6. **LESSON:** "Push hard: 5 to 6 centimetres deep. Push fast: 100 to 120 a minute. Let the chest come all the way back up. And don't stop."

   - *On screen:* app screen showing a live rate/depth score

## 6. Before posting: checklist

- [ ] Beat 4: count the quiz comments on the last part, then pick the vote, shout-out or no-vote version (section 2c). Replace `[XX]%` with real numbers only.
- [ ] A doctor or the client checks every medical line, especially:
  - "up to 10% per minute" (AHA: 7–10% per minute without CPR)
  - "5–6 cm, 100–120/min, full recoil"
  - switching every ~2 min
  - AED steps
  - choking: 5 back blows + 5 abdominal thrusts, and the self-rescue method
  - "bystander CPR can double survival"
- [ ] Confirm the emergency number with the client: 112 (all-in-one, India) or 108 (ambulance). Use one consistently.
- [ ] Confirm with HaloCPR whether the app is free for individuals before saying "free" anywhere.
- [ ] Never say "get certified". HaloCPR gives a training record, not an accredited certification.
- [ ] Set up the comment-to-DM tool on the keyword HALO, then test it from a second account before the first reel goes live.
- [ ] Get the HaloCPR logo and an app screen recording (needed for Parts 2 and 5).
- [ ] Timing: read each script aloud. It must land under 55 s, so trim story lines first if it runs over.
- [ ] Bio mismatch: the page bio says "explained in 40 seconds" but reels run up to 55 s. Update the bio or trim the scripts.
